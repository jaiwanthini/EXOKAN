6. Why CANDIDATE remains difficult:
The physical measurements between transit false positives (e.g. grazing eclipsing binaries) and small planetary candidates often overlap in identical density distributions in this 14-feature space. Standardized mean differences show minimal separation power.

7. Why the final model is leakage-controlled:
All thresholds, hierarchical groupings, and focal loss parameters were strictly validated on an 80/20 train/val split entirely separated from the pristine test set. The preprocessing pipelines (Imputation, Scaling, Target transformations) were fitted explicitly and exclusively on the training folds.

8. Why KAN is useful compared with Random Forest:
Despite intense tabular noise, KAN's symbolic spline interactions mathematically parsed the engineered ratios to slightly outperform the 300-estimator Random Forest baseline (78.7% vs 78.0%). KAN offers an intrinsically interpretable layer-wise structure (symbolic distillation) impossible with opaque forest ensembles.
